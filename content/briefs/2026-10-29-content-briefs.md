## 1. Turks & Caicos: hoteles todo incluido

- **Queries:** hoteles todo incluido en islas turcas y caicos (123 impr., pos. 27.0), resorts todo incluido en islas turcas y caicos (30, 26.1), resorts all inclusive en islas turcas y caicos (20, 21.2).
- **Intent:** commercial. The searcher wants a short list of all-inclusive options.
- **Page:** new H2 on the Turks & Caicos hub (`content/islas-turcas-y-caicos/`), not a new URL. One page should hold the topic so the hub does not compete with itself.
- **Angle:** honesty. Very few luxury hotels in the islands sell an all-inclusive plan; say so, name only the ones that do, and explain the alternatives (meal plans, dining credit from the perks).
- **H2:** ¿Hay hoteles todo incluido de lujo en Islas Turcas y Caicos?
- **Outline:**
  1. Direct answer (40 to 60 words): which hotels in the collection offer an all-inclusive plan, confirmed with each hotel.
  2. Candidates to check: Ambergris Cay (hotel 6860, page name "All Inclusive"; see `ambergris-cay-corrected-text.md`), Every other hotel: confirm whether a meal plan exists.
  3. Alternatives: half board or meal plans where offered; the daily breakfast for two and the hotel credit from Solo5Estrellas.
  4. FAQ entry: "¿Qué incluye un plan todo incluido en Islas Turcas y Caicos?" (answer only with confirmed facts).
- **Facts to source:** plan availability per hotel (hotel websites or the supplier), what the plan includes.
- **Internal links:** hotel pages of the confirmed hotels; Caribe region page.
- **Schema:** add the question to the hub's FAQPage.
- **Length:** 150 to 250 words.
- **Measure:** position for the three queries (baseline 21 to 27), clicks.

## 2. Turks & Caicos: hoteles para luna de miel

- **Queries:** hoteles para luna de miel en islas turcas y caicos (66, pos. 21.4), hoteles para luna de miel en turks and caicos (48, 23.7).
- **Intent:** commercial, high value (long stays, suites).
- **Page:** expand the "Luna de miel" row of the hub's "Qué hotel elegir" table into an H2.
- **H2:** Hoteles para luna de miel en Islas Turcas y Caicos
- **Outline:**
  1. Direct answer naming 4 to 5 hotels already in the table (Wymara, Rock House, Grace Bay Club villas, COMO Parrot Cay, Amanyara) and the reason for each in one line.
  2. What to ask for: suites with private pool, adults-only areas (Grace Bay Club adult wing), private dinners. Only list features confirmed on the hotel pages.
  3. When to go (link to the "Cuándo viajar" section, no repetition).
  4. Honeymoon offers: only if a hotel publishes one; otherwise say the advisor can ask.
- **Facts to source:** adults-only policies, suite types, any published honeymoon packages.
- **Internal links:** the five hotel pages; "Hoteles para luna de miel" in other destinations later (calendar).
- **Length:** 200 to 300 words.
- **Measure:** position (baseline 21 to 24).

## 3. Turks & Caicos: condo hoteles y residencias

- **Query:** condo hoteles en turks and caicos (154, pos. 18.7). The largest single sub-intent in the cluster.
- **Intent:** commercial. "Condo hotel" means suites or residences with a kitchen, usually privately owned and rented through the resort.
- **Page:** new H2 on the hub.
- **H2:** Condo hoteles y residencias con cocina en Islas Turcas y Caicos
- **Outline:**
  1. Short definition (what a condo hotel is, in plain words).
  2. Hotels in the collection with kitchen suites or residences: West Bay Club (suites with kitchen, per the hub table), Ritz-Carlton Residences, The Shore Club (villas and beach houses), The Palms (suites), Grace Bay Club villas. Confirm kitchens per hotel.
  3. Who it suits: families, long stays, groups.
  4. Perks on residence bookings: the hub shows "Consulte los beneficios" for Ritz-Carlton Residences; confirm before stating anything.
- **Facts to source:** kitchen or kitchenette per suite type, minimum stays.
- **Length:** 150 to 250 words.
- **Measure:** position (baseline 18.7).

## 9. Guide: qué son los beneficios de reservar con un asesor

- **Why:** the perks (breakfast for two, hotel credit, upgrade, late check-out) are the site's unique angle against OTAs and brand sites, but no page explains them in Spanish. Brand and hotel pages can link to one guide instead of repeating it.
- **Working title:** "Beneficios de reservar hoteles de lujo con Solo5Estrellas" (H1); title ≤60.
- **Outline:** what you get and when it applies; same rate as booking direct (only if the client confirms); programmes behind the perks (Four Seasons Preferred Partner, Marriott STARS and Luminous, Hyatt Privé, Leading Hotels, Accor HERA) named once, without logos; conditions (upgrade subject to availability, minimum nights on some hotels); FAQ.
- **Facts to source:** programme names and conditions from the client; no claims of savings or prices.
- **Internal links:** from every brand page, the hotel template perks block and the home page.
- **Length:** 800 to 1,200 words.
- **Dependency:** client approval of the wording.
