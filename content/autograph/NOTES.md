## Source
Perks, programme wording and the hotel list come from our live collection page: 158 hotels in 35 countries by the list; the text says 152 and 33 (Z42). Autograph Collection is Marriott's portfolio of independent hotels, and Marriott Luminous covers it (One Mile at a Time, Upgraded Points). The page's perks differ from other Luminous brands: no early check-in is listed, late check-out is on request, and Wi-Fi is "básico". No prices, ratings or "best" claims. This is the chain with the most Spanish hotels in the collection; the Spain block opens by default and the FAQ lists six of them.
