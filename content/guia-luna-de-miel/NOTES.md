**Why:** Turks & Caicos honeymoon queries had 114 impressions at positions 21 to 24 (Search Console, 26 Jul to 25 Oct 2026). Honeymoon is a high-value trip type for the Mexico, Spain and US Hispanic markets, and the site had no honeymoon page.
