# SEO audit: solo5estrellas.com (29 October 2026)

**Scope:** full site audit. **Data:** Search Console export of 27 Oct (web, 26 Jul to 25 Oct 2026, top 1,000 queries and pages), the Search Console API pull of 18 Oct (29 Jan to 18 Oct), live checks of eight templates on 29 Oct, `verify.sh` (44 checks), and web search for competitors. **Not available:** keyword volume, difficulty and backlink data. Ahrefs answers "Insufficient plan", Semrush has no API units and SEO Gets lists the site as Free. Difficulty below is judged from the search results, not measured.

## Executive summary
The site is visible but not chosen: Google showed it about 45,400 times in three months and it earned 94 clicks (CTR 0.21%), at an average position of 18.6. Its biggest strength is breadth: around 3,200 hotels across 62 brand collections and 130+ countries, and real ranking pages for brand and destination searches (Renaissance and The Mayflower at position 9.6, Taj at 12.5, Rocco Forte at 8.7, Turks & Caicos at 13.4). The top three priorities are: (1) fix the shared page head — there are no canonical tags, a broken meta tag hides the description, and city and country pages are titled "Bienvenido"; (2) give every hotel, brand and region page one URL — 650 page IDs answer under several URLs and hold about 45% of all impressions; (3) publish the Spanish copy already drafted for the 62 brand, 12 region and 131 country pages. **Assessment: critical issues** in the technical foundation; the content to fix it is ready and waiting on the developer.

## On-page issues table
Checked on 29 Oct: home, a brand page, a hotel page, a city page, a country page, a region page.

| Page | Issue | Severity | Recommended fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All templates | No canonical tag on any page checked | Critical | One self-referencing canonical per page (fix 2 in `developer-spec/IN-PLACE-FIXES.md`) |
| All templates | Unterminated `<meta name="title" content="` swallows the description and cuts the `hreflang="es"` tag | Critical | Remove the meta title tag; output one valid description (fix 1) |
| City and country pages | `<title>` is "Bienvenido \| Solo 5 Estrellas por Lorraine Travel" (London, Spain checked; all 134 country pages) | Critical | Template titles from `developer-spec/page-metadata-drafts.csv` |
| Brand pages | English title ("Ritz Carlton Hotels & Resorts under Marriott's STARS Program Preferred Partner \| …"), 100+ characters, typo "Excusive" | High | Spanish title ≤60 characters (drafted for all 62) |
| Home, region, country pages | H1 and title make "Los Mejores Hoteles…" claims | Medium | Factual H1 ("Hoteles de lujo en Europa: 981 hoteles en 39 países") |
| Brand and city pages | Images without alt text: 60 of 61 on the Ritz-Carlton page, 81 of 82 on the London page | Medium | Alt text from the hotel name and city |
| Brand, region, country pages | Counts typed by hand disagree with the list (about 14 brand pages, both region pages checked) | Medium | Generate counts from the list |
| Hotel pages | Hotel names with markup (`<br>`, asterisks) and "br" in nine URLs | Medium | Clean names and slugs (Z101) |
| Region and country pages | Breadcrumb links to legacy `browse_region.cfm` | Medium | Link to `/regions/{id}/` |
| All templates | URL slugs carry programme names and mixed case (`Bvlgari_under_The_Marriott_STARS_Program.html`) | High | One lowercase Spanish slug per ID with 301s (fix 4) |
