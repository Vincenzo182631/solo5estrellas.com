# Programme confirmation pack (draft, 17 October 2026)

**What this is.** Our chain and hotel pages state perks, programme names and hotel lists that no public source confirms. This pack gives one short email per programme, with the exact claims our pages make and the questions that close the open findings. Nothing has been sent. Contacts are not in the repo, so each email needs a recipient (your account manager at each programme, or the advisor-support desk). Send one email per programme; a single thread to everyone would not get answered.

**What we need back, every time** (so one reply closes the finding): the programme's current name; the perks it gives an authorised advisor, with amounts; whether points and elite credit still earn; which hotels take part; and the date the information is valid.

## Email template (English)
> **Subject:** Solo5Estrellas / Lorraine Travel: confirming our [PROGRAMME] perks wording
>
> Hello [NAME],
>
> Lorraine Travel (agency since 1948) markets [BRAND/PROGRAMME] hotels in Spanish at solo5estrellas.com. Before we publish new pages, I want to make sure we describe the programme correctly. Could you confirm:
>
> 1. The current official name of the advisor programme, and our agency's participation.
> 2. The perks included for our clients, with amounts: [DAILY BREAKFAST FOR TWO / HOTEL CREDIT / UPGRADE / EARLY CHECK-IN AND LATE CHECK-OUT / WELCOME GIFT / WI-FI].
> 3. Whether guests still earn [LOYALTY PROGRAMME] points and elite credit on these bookings.
> 4. Which hotels in the list below take part, or a link to the participating list.
> 5. Any minimum stay or blackout rule.
> 6. The date this applies from.
>
> A short written reply is enough. Thank you,
> [NAME], Lorraine Travel
